Record once. Turn it into a demo or a guide.

Screvio records what you do in the browser — screen video plus every real click, key press and page change — on one timeline, so a session can be edited into a polished demo or published as a step-by-step guide without recording it again.

Screvio Recorder

Browser extension. Records a tab, window, screen or region together with the actions behind it. Passwords and card fields are masked.

Screvio Studio

Trim, zoom in on clicks, add voice-over and subtitles, and export MP4 or Word documents.

Screvio Player

Browser extension. Shows your team's guides as highlighted steps on the web apps they already use. It records nothing.

Recordings stay on your computer unless you export them or publish a guide to your organization's own Screvio server. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
