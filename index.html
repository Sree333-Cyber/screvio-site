Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are my recordings uploaded anywhere?

No. Recordings are stored in your own browser. They only leave your computer when you export a file yourself, or publish a guide to your organization's Screvio server.

Do I need a server to use Screvio?

Recording works on its own. Editing, voice-over and export happen in Screvio Studio, which opens in your browser and is connected to a Screvio server — the same server that lets a team share one library of guides.

Which browsers are supported?

Screvio Recorder and Screvio Player are available for Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Mozilla Firefox.

Can I change a video without recording it again?

Yes. Trim, zoom, click highlights, narration and subtitles are all edits laid over the original recording, which is always kept unchanged.

Does Screvio Player record my screen?

No. The Player only shows guides. It has no screen-recording or activity-capture ability at all, and asks for far fewer browser permissions than the Recorder.

What can I export?

MP4 video, subtitle files (.srt and .vtt), Word documents with a screenshot per step, test-case spreadsheets, and the original recording.
